# My editing style

- Language: Filipino / Taglish; retain accurate product and brand spelling.
- Default format: vertical 1080x1920, 30 fps.
- Pace: natural, trim unnecessary pauses without cutting words/phrases.
- Captions: readable white text with shadow; short phrases, clear of platform UI.
- Product: preserve actual appearance; use supplied facts only.
- Hook: clear in the first 1–3 seconds, grounded in the footage and brief.
- Sound: voiceover first, subtle licensed music, purposeful light SFX.
- Motion: restrained, no unnecessary flashes or distorted product images.
- CTA: leave lower space for platform overlays; use brief's exact CTA.
- Transcription: local faster-whisper unless I explicitly choose another provider.
